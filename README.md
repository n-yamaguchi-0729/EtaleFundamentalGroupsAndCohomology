## Goal and coverage

The goal is a complete formalization of the following Stacks Project chapters:

- **Étale fundamental groups:** [58 — Fundamental Groups of Schemes](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0BQ6).
- **Étale cohomology:** [59 — Étale Cohomology](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03N1), [63 — More Étale Cohomology](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F4U), and [64 — The Trace Formula](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0F5P).

Coverage below refers to the published source, checked on 2026-10-13.
**Formalized** means the full numbered statement is covered; **Remaining**
includes partial results and statements whose full correspondence has not yet
been verified. **Lean** links to this repository; **Mathlib** links to its pinned
upstream source. Standalone equation and notation numbers are omitted; **text** links to
sections without numbered statements. Unnumbered assertions also remain within
the goal, and no chapter is complete.
